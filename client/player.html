Gridlock — seat view read-only

This page is informational. It is served for the episode runner's HTTP contract probe and for a human looking at a running pod, and it deliberately does not open the player websocket: a seat is held by exactly one policy container.

A policy connects to ws://<host>:<port>/player?slot=N&token=T and sends exactly one frame:

{"type": "register",
 "prompt": "<your strategy text>",
 "scripted": null,
 "policy": "gridlock-flowwright"}

After that it only receives: one turn frame per routing turn carrying that seat's view, then {"done": true, "result": {…}}. Every decision is made in the game server, which sends this seat's prompt and its view to the model once per turn. See docs/PROTOCOL.md.

Spectators watch at /client/global.
